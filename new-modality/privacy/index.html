Privacy Policy

new-modality for Mac and the new-modality remote for iPhone · Last updated November 17, 2026

In short: your voice is transcribed on your own Mac and is never sent to us or anyone else. We don't run analytics, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you. The only thing that leaves your Mac about you is a license check, if you buy Pro.

This policy covers new-modality, the Mac app, and new-modality remote, the iPhone app that controls it. "We" means Banji Oyewole, who makes both.

The Mac app

Your voice and what you dictate

  • Speech is turned into text on your Mac, by a speech model running on your Mac. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Each recording and its transcript are saved on your Mac, in the app's folder in Application Support, so you can find past dictations. By default they are deleted after 7 days. You can keep them longer, or not save them at all, on the Dictation page of the app's settings.
  • Nothing said while a password field has focus is saved.

What the app reads on your Mac

To type, click and run commands for you, the app uses the Accessibility, Input Monitoring and (optionally) Screen Recording permissions macOS asks you to grant. It reads window titles, the names of buttons and menu items, and text on screen, so it can do what you ask and spell names correctly. This stays on your Mac.

Optional features that use other software

  • Automatic punctuation sends the text of a dictation to a language model server for punctuation. By default that is Apple's on-device model, on your Mac. If you set it to use another computer, the text goes to that computer, which you choose and control.
  • Agentic Control sends your request, and a description of what is on screen, to an Ollama model server. By default that is on your Mac; you can point it at another computer you control.
  • Raycast and Siri, if you use them through the app, receive the text you speak to them. Their own privacy policies apply.

We never receive any of this.

Downloads

On first launch the app downloads its speech model from Hugging Face. Like any download, the server sees your IP address. No account or identifier of yours is sent.

Buying Pro and checking your license

  • Purchases are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. Polar collects your email address and payment details to sell you the license and handle tax, receipts and refunds, under Polar's privacy policy. We receive your email address and what you bought; we never see your card details.
  • When you enter a license key, the app sends the key and your Mac's computer name to Polar to register this Mac. The computer name is there so you can recognise the Mac in Polar's customer portal and free its slot. Once a day after that, the app sends the key and the ID Polar gave this Mac to check the license is still valid.
  • Nothing you say, type or dictate is sent with a license check. If you never enter a key, the app never contacts Polar.

The iPhone app

  • new-modality remote collects no data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • It talks only to the Mac you pair it with. The connection is encrypted, and a phone that hasn't been paired can't connect.
  • Microphone: used only if you choose the phone as the microphone. Audio goes straight to your Mac, encrypted, to be transcribed there.
  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing code your Mac shows. No image is kept or sent.
  • Local network and Bluetooth: used only to find and connect to your Mac.
  • Your pairing and settings are stored on the phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports from either app.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information.

Children

Neither app is directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete saved recordings and transcripts at any time from the app's folder, or set the app to keep none.
  • Remove a license from a Mac on the License page of the app's settings, which frees it in Polar.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have your purchase record deleted, write to us at the address below. Polar's records are also covered by their own policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change means more of your data leaves your devices, the apps will tell you before it takes effect.
