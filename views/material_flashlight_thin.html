FLASHLIGHT X
The intersection of form and function.

I built Flashlight X because flashlight apps frustrated me. The Play Store was full of apps that either tried to copy a physical flashlight on screen or buried a simple tool under ads. I wanted something lightweight that felt like it belonged on Android, so I designed it for the screen instead of imitating the real object.

The result is one button that fills the whole screen. A Floating Action Button opens everything else: strobe, a mode that reacts to sound, and a live camera preview. Each feature is one tap away, and none of them slow you down when you just need light.
